# 📊 Salaries & Compensation Analysis Dashboard

An interactive HR Analytics dashboard designed to evaluate salary distributions, compensation structures, experience-pay correlations, and workforce demographics across **500 employees** with a total payroll budget of **$4.96 Million**.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project provides a comprehensive data-driven view of organizational compensation. The dashboard enables HR leaders, compensation specialists, and business executives to analyze salary parity, evaluate geographic pay variations, examine education vs. salary dynamics, and track experience-based salary growth.

---

## 🔑 Key Metrics & KPI Summary

| Metric | Value | Details |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Total Payroll Budget** | **$4.96M** | Combined annual compensation across all active headcount |
| **Total Workforce** | **500** | Full sample employee population analyzed |
| **Geographic Reach** | **7 Locations** | Philadelphia, Denver, Fort Worth, Houston, San Jose, Seattle, Indianapolis |
| **Organizational Coverage** | **7 Departments** | Marketing, Operations, R&D, IT, HR, Sales, Finance |
| **Gender Distribution** | **53% Female / 47% Male** | Balanced across both total salary payout and employee headcount |

---

## 🎯 Key Features & Dashboard Visualizations

1. **Executive KPI Header**: Instant visibility into overall payroll spending ($4.96M), total employee count (500), active locations (7), and organizational departments (7).
2. **Salary vs. Experience Correlation**: Trend plot modeling compensation trajectory against years of experience to identify pay growth scales and potential outlier compensations.
3. **Geographic Salary Breakdown**: Comparative bar charts showcasing average salaries across key metropolitan locations (led by Philadelphia and Denver).
4. **Compensation by Education & Department**: Analysis mapping average base pay across academic qualifications (*High School, BSc, MSc, PhD*) and functional business units (*Marketing, R&D, IT, HR, Operations, Sales, Finance*).
5. **Top 10 Roles by Compensation**: Ranked salary benchmark visual highlighting top-earning roles including *Marketing Executive*, *Operations Manager*, *R&D Manager*, and *Data Scientist*.
6. **Gender Parity & Pay Equity Analysis**: Dual donut charts displaying gender ratio breakdowns for total salary allocation vs. employee headcount (47% Male, 53% Female).
7. **Interactive Slicers & Filtering System**: Multi-layered filter pane allowing dynamic drill-downs by:
   - **Department**: Marketing, Operations, R&D, Sales, Finance, IT, HR
   - **Job Title**: Account Manager, Accountant, Data Scientist, HR Manager, etc.
   - **Seniority Level**: Entry Level, Mid Level, Manager

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used

- **Data Analytics & Visualization**: Power BI / Excel
- **Data Modeling & Transformation**: Power Query / DAX / Pandas
- **Data Source**: HR Payroll & Employee Demographics Dataset

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- **Pay Equity**: Salary allocation perfectly mirrors headcount representation across genders (53% Female / 47% Male), demonstrating strong equitable pay practices across the organization.
- **Top Compensation Drivers**: Executive and management roles in Marketing, Operations, and R&D lead the organization in base salary scales.
- **Experience Trajectory**: Compensation shows a strong positive linear trend relative to years of experience, with steeper pay increases observed beyond 15+ years of tenure.
